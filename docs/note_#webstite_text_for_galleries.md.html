#Photography
Gallery 1 – Land & Light
Description: Wide skies, shifting light, and open fields. These are the moments where the landscape feels endless and human noise fades away. Taken across the UK, often in that quiet hour before or after the weather turns.
Captions (examples):
Evening sun over Cambourne fields, the light lasting just long enough for one frame.
Rapeseed gold under a fast-moving spring sky.
A storm retreating, leaving behind a faint double rainbow.
The last glow cutting through farmland — five minutes later, gone.

Gallery 2 – Stillness
Description: Silence has a texture. Water, wind, and distance — nothing much happens here, which is the point.
Captions:
Swans at first light on a motionless lake.
Marker post, sea, and the weight of a still horizon.
Boats waiting for weather that never came.
Morning reflection — not a breath of wind, not a sound.

Gallery 3 – Weather & Drama
Description: Nature showing off. Rain, lightning, and the kind of light that can’t decide what it wants to be.
Captions:
Lightning strikes beyond the fields — one frame, pure luck.
Dark skies rolling in over pylons and wheat.
Half the sky sunlight, half the sky fury.
Rain sweeping the valley, everything alive in that chaos.

Gallery 4 – Human Stories
Description: People leave traces — footprints, laughter, the occasional Jeep restoration. This gallery catches the moments where we appear and vanish again.
Captions:
Old mechanics, old jokes, same stubborn tyre.
Cottage window — lace, models, and quiet pride.
The telescope at sunset, still listening for something out there.
A path built by hand, disappearing into dunes and memory.

Gallery 5 – Coast & Edge
Description: The meeting place of land and sea. Constant motion, constant erosion — calm one day, violent the next.
Captions:
High tide approaching a lone marker.
Pebbles, cliffs, and lines that refuse to stay straight.
A boardwalk to nowhere, wind cutting through the grass.
The light always different, the sound always the same.

Gallery 6 – Seasons & Change
Description: The slow turn of things — winter mist, autumn colour, and those brief hours when the light hits differently.
Captions:
A fogged woodland, colour fighting through grey.
The last leaves before frost.
Summer fields stretched out and buzzing with life.
A park tree catching the first light of day.
